The Original Steakhouse

Since 2003, Talindo Steak House has stood as a beacon of fine dining in Lagos. We introduced a generation to the art of the perfect steak—aged, grilled, and served with uncompromising attention to detail.

Our intimate dining room on Karimu Kotun Street offers a quiet sanctuary for those who appreciate exceptional beef, a curated wine list, and service that anticipates your every need. We are not just a restaurant; we are a legacy.
